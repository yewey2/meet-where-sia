Privacy

Last updated 26 July 2026

Meet Where Sia is a meeting-point planning tool. You can use a local plan without an account, or create a shared plan that anyone with its link can view.

Local plans

Names and locations entered into a local plan are stored in your browser’s local storage so the plan can be restored on the same device. Clearing the plan removes that browser copy.

Shared plans

When you create or join a shared plan, the app stores the plan name, participant names and locations, contributor usernames, roles, update times and password hashes in a managed Redis database. Only the owner provides an email address. Plaintext passwords are not stored. A secure, HTTP-only session cookie keeps editors signed in for up to 30 days.

Anyone with a shared-plan link can view its participant names and locations without logging in. Contributors can edit only their assigned participant. The owner can manage all participants and contributor accounts, reset passwords and permanently delete the plan. Removing access blocks that contributor’s existing session from making changes.

Map and location services

When Google features are enabled, searches and map requests are sent to Google Maps Platform so that addresses, suggestions, coordinates and map tiles can be returned. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. When the backup map is used, map tile requests are sent to OpenStreetMap.

Service providers and logs

The server requests public station data from Singapore government sources and, when configured, train-service status from LTA DataMall. Vercel hosts the app and Upstash stores shared-plan data. These providers may retain standard security and request logs such as IP address, browser information and request time under their own policies.

Your choices

You can use exact MRT/LRT station names without Google place search, keep a plan local, clear local data at any time, sign out of a shared plan, ask its owner to remove your access, or have the owner delete the shared plan.
